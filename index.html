02  /  For builders

The sub you stop having to chase.

Most electrical problems on a build are not electrical. They are a crew that did not show, a rough-in nobody photographed, and a question that took three days to get answered.

  • 01We hold the date Rough-in and trim are booked against your schedule, and you hear from us before the date moves, not after.
  • 02Photos of the rough-in when you need them Rough-ins are photographed and filed against the lot before drywall. If a question comes up later about what is behind a wall, ask and we will send you the picture.
  • 03One number, one answer You are not routed through an office. You ask the person who did the work.
  • 04We stand behind our own work at inspection Plenty of jurisdictions require you or the homeowner to pull the permit rather than us. Whoever holds it, we meet the inspector on our work, and any correction on it is put right on our time.
